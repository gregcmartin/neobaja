# Decisions to make with Greg

These choices were not settled clearly enough to hard-code into a restart.
They do not block a one-stage vertical slice.

- Confirm the final title and whether the public product may use the Polaris,
  Can-Am, SCORE, and OutRun names or needs original fictional equivalents.
- Confirm stage order. The rejected build used Ensenada, San Felipe, Valle de
  Trinidad, then Bahia de los Angeles.
- Choose the championship structure: four sequential legs, separate courses,
  branching routes, or another format.
- Choose target race length, time limits/checkpoints, difficulty progression,
  continue/lives behavior, and whether position or time is the main goal.
- Decide whether an attract/demo mode is wanted. Default for the restart is no
  demo mode.
- Define audio direction: music style, engine sound, terrain/contact effects,
  crowd/ambient effects, and voice samples.
- Decide whether two-player or alternating-player support is in scope.
- Choose whether vehicle upgrades, damage, boost, jumps, shortcuts, weather
  effects, and route hazards are core or later features.
- Confirm the target release: MAME-first prototype, AES/MVS ROM, physical cart,
  or all three.
- Approve a new engine/toolchain choice before implementation. This packet does
  not carry the rejected engine forward.
